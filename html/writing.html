The narrow apartment is quiet, lit only by the orange glow of a desk lamp. Papers, sketches, and half-empty coffee cups clutter the table. LANA (28), tired but focused, flips through her notebook.

The sound of distant traffic hums through the window. She pauses, stares at a blank page, then takes a slow breath — searching for the right words. Her fingers tremble just slightly.

A soft knock at the door breaks the silence. Lana freezes, unsure, then closes the notebook gently and stands up.
